For the contractor clients you advise, project records can turn a broad risk discussion into practical questions. Given your construction and transportation focus at Watkins, Liberty Mutual’s construction quality-control checklist offers a useful starting point.

Its ten steps can help you explore questions such as:

  • Who owns each quality-control responsibility before work starts?

  • Which records show completed work was reviewed?

Use the prompts alongside your own discovery process to identify topics that deserve a closer look. Bring the answers into a discussion about the operation, the project and the documentation available for review.
