BRAND KNOWLEDGE / 01

Purpose and audience

# Purpose and audience

Help independent agents place business, retain clients and finish the next useful piece of work.

The relationship we support

The portal serves independent agencies and their people. It must respect their expertise, time and freedom to place business with more than one insurer. Different roles at the same agency need different emphasis without a different navigation model.

Liberty Mutual describes agents as a source of personalized guidance and help with policy service and renewals. Its commercial dashboard provides distinct paths for small commercial, midsize and large brokers, surety, and wholesale specialty. [LM02, LM04]

The current brand context

Liberty Mutual announced that the Safeco brand retirement took effect April 25, 2026. The current personal-lines experience therefore uses Liberty Mutual branding. Independent-agent and direct-channel offerings remain differentiated; consumer advertising must not be converted into agent-channel coverage promises. [LM03]

Proposed experience promise

**Clarity for your work. Context for your clients. A team for what comes next.**

This is original portal positioning. It expresses the experience we intend to build, without creating an underwriting promise, service-level commitment or official corporate slogan.

Role | What earns relevance

Agency principal | A clear account view, meaningful reporting period and practical growth actions.

Producer | Business and state guidance, organized requirements and an understandable submission path.

Account manager | Renewal priorities, client context, documents and a reliable follow-up.

Specialist / broker | Accurate parties, channel identity, requirements and specialist-review context.

BRAND KNOWLEDGE / 02

Voice that respects the agent

# Voice that respects the agent

Knowledgeable. Direct. Supportive.

Write for a capable professional

Use the vocabulary the task needs, and explain unfamiliar terms on first use. Put the next action near the reason it matters. Keep headings short, sentence case and specific. Prefer useful verbs: review, prepare, save, submit, assign and contact.

Intent | Preferred portal copy

Start work | “Prepare your next submission.”

Show a saved state | “Draft saved. Continue when you are ready.”

Explain missing information | “Add the property details to continue.”

Confirm a request | “Request received. Follow its progress in the activity timeline.”

Support a question | “Send this question to your relationship team.”

Invite learning | “Build confidence in your next small-business conversation.”

Precision in outcomes

Name the state that actually occurred. A draft is saved; a submission is received; a referral is sent for review. None of these statements means coverage is bound, a policy is issued or a bond is in force. Do not claim an action reached a live external insurer system unless that integration is verified.

Respectful personalization

Explain relevance through the work: “Resources for Illinois employers” or “Continue your commercial learning path.” Keep private production figures out of campaign copy. Avoid language that labels an agency as weak, behind, low-value or underperforming.

Errors help people recover

State the problem, preserve entered work where possible and offer the next useful action. Do not expose technical exceptions, tokens or platform names in agent-facing errors.

BRAND KNOWLEDGE / 04

Logo, imagery and interface language

# Logo, imagery and interface language

Use authentic identity. Let useful work lead the page.

Logo handling

Use the provided official horizontal mark intact in the portal header. Keep its proportions, legibility and breathing room. Use the supplied symbol only where the compact setting makes the full mark impractical. Do not redraw, stretch, outline or combine it with an invented lockup. Retain the source SVGs and asset provenance.

Standalone files preserve the original SVG path geometry and supply explicit dimensions and the original light-theme styles. The standalone logo omits the alternate dark-theme group that is hidden on the source light page. The verbatim source SVG is retained alongside it. Trademark ownership remains with Liberty Mutual; this pack does not grant a separate redistribution license.

Photography direction

Prefer natural moments of professional collaboration and recognizable work environments. Pair the image with the content: an agency conversation for learning, a retail business for small commercial, a job site for construction, or an organized office for professional services. Avoid disaster spectacle, stereotyped people and staged handshakes as a default visual language.

Do not generate or repurpose photography as evidence of an actual Liberty Mutual employee, agency, insured, office or event. Record the source and usage status of each photograph before publication. The initial asset pack intentionally contains no third-party stock photography.

A deliberate everyday workspace

&#8226; Place priorities, status and next actions above editorial promotion.

&#8226; Keep seven stable destinations; personalize emphasis and recommendations.

&#8226; Use status labels and icons together. Color alone must not carry meaning.

&#8226; Use editorial imagery on product, growth and learning pages; keep operational tables focused.

&#8226; Prefer concise empty states, visible filter context and clear back navigation.

BRAND KNOWLEDGE / 05

Content organized around the work

# Content organized around the work

One vocabulary across reusable pages, Search and campaigns.

Family | Representative content and work

Personal | Auto/home renewal preparation, household change questions and follow-up.

Small commercial | BOP preparation, workers compensation state context, commercial auto discovery.

Farm and ranch | Operation and property information, seasonal context and specialist referral.

Midsize / large commercial | Multi-location packages, missing requirements and specialist questions.

Retail specialty | Complex exposures, appropriate distribution path and focused referral.

Surety | Principal, obligee, bond request, supporting requirements and servicing.

Wholesale specialty | Retail partner context, wholesale review and placement handoff.

Metadata is part of the content

Each resource needs a stable ID and slug, title, summary, body, business family, product, state applicability, distribution channel, resource type, language, source references and review date. Do not infer state applicability from the agent's home state when the risk is elsewhere.

Search labels

Use clear filters such as Business line, Product, Risk state, Resource type and Language. Results should show enough context to choose a resource: title, short summary, state, type and review date. A search result must not expose account-private material simply because the visitor is known.

Claims and availability

A product taxonomy helps people navigate. It does not establish appointment, eligibility, availability, pricing or policy terms. Those decisions belong to authorized operational services and reviewed product guidance. Product summaries stay distinct from the synthetic appetite matrix.

BRAND KNOWLEDGE / 06

State guidance with a source

# State guidance with a source

Use the risk location and preserve the qualifications.

Texas

Texas generally does not require workers compensation for most private employers. Coverage is required for employees working on government contracts, and other contracts may introduce requirements. The employer's situation and non-subscriber obligations need review against the Texas Department of Insurance guidance. [TX01]

Florida

Florida requirements depend on industry, employee count and entity organization. Construction, non-construction and agriculture have different rules. Owner exemptions, out-of-state work and contractor obligations need context; a single employee-count badge is insufficient. [FL01]

Illinois

Illinois generally requires coverage from the first employee, including part-time employees, with rare statutory exceptions. Owner opt-outs and extra-hazardous occupations require specific review. The Commission's insurance guidance is the starting source, not a substitute for applying the law to the employer. [IL01]

How the portal presents this content

Lead with the jurisdiction, label the article as state guidance, show a source review date and link directly to the regulator. Keep the summary educational. Do not convert it into automated legal advice or a Liberty Mutual underwriting rule. Refresh time-sensitive content before a scheduled review date and after a material source change.

BRAND KNOWLEDGE / 07

A campaign for the agency account

# A campaign for the agency account

Expand your small-business practice

The proposed campaign objective

Help an established personal-lines agency develop confidence in an appropriate small-commercial conversation. Use a learning path, BOP preparation checklist and appetite inquiry as useful next actions. The account is the agency; its principal, producer and account manager remain separate contacts.

Contact | Proposed message and next action

Principal | “Build your next chapter in small business.” Review the learning path and choose an agency action.

Producer | “Bring a stronger submission to the table.” Prepare the business story and BOP request.

Account manager | “Connect everyday conversations to new needs.” Learn discovery questions and involve the right teammate.

Neutral fallback | “Resources for your next client conversation.” Browse guidance, checklists and learning.

Inputs and boundaries

Use a dated, fictional agency cohort with explicit production-period definitions and selection rules. Limited commercial production is a campaign hypothesis, not proof of demand, suitability or an underwriting opportunity. Use supplied scenario facts for fictional agencies; do not invent company news or scrape unrelated businesses for enrichment.

The native Agentic Studio flow should receive this Brand Kit, the account brief, approved source resources, permitted CTA and role-specific context. Generated agency briefs and cohort files remain internal. Only reviewed, reusable content and promotions move into CMS publication and Search.

What a good result looks like

A concise account objective, useful role variants, accurate terminology, no unsupported benefits and an actionable learning or preparation CTA. Measure actual engagement. Do not present a few walkthrough visits as statistically proven lift.

BRAND KNOWLEDGE / 08

A precise shared glossary

# A precise shared glossary

Preserve meaning across content and translation.

Term | Portal usage

Agency / account | The independent business relationship. In ABM, agency is the account; employees are contacts.

Agent / producer | An individual insurance professional. Role and permissions are explicit; the terms are not universal access levels.

Named insured | A person or entity identified in the applicable policy. Do not substitute the logged-in agent.

Policyholder | A holder of a policy; use the applicable record and policy context.

Risk state | The jurisdiction of the risk or work under discussion. It may differ from the agent's office state.

Businessowners policy (BOP) | Spell out on first use. Use “BOP” thereafter in the same context.

Workers compensation | Portal product label. Preserve an official document's title when linking to it.

Appetite | Guidance about risks considered; not a guarantee of acceptance or authority to bind.

Submission / referral | A request for review. A status change alone does not bind coverage.

Surety principal | The party whose obligation is backed by the bond; distinguish from an agency principal.

Obligee | The party to whom the principal owes the obligation in the bond context.

Bond request | A request requiring review; distinguish from an issued bond.

Retail / wholesale | Distribution context; not a measure of agency size or sophistication.

These are proposed editorial usage rules, not policy definitions. Preserve Liberty Mutual, Ironshore, SitecoreAI and source document titles as named entities. Translate surrounding language deliberately; require a qualified insurance-content reviewer for translated coverage and legal terminology.

BRAND KNOWLEDGE / 09

Guidance for content generation

# Guidance for content generation

Create a useful draft with a visible path to review.

Supply the right context

&#8226; Name the audience, role, task, risk state, business family, content type and permitted CTA.

&#8226; Provide the approved source resource IDs and the review date.

&#8226; Specify the word count and output fields the component needs.

&#8226; Keep private client, agency production and authentication data out of published content.

Stay within evidence

Use only supplied source facts for product, channel and regulatory claims. When a fact is missing, identify the question for editorial review. Do not invent policy limits, discounts, appointment rules, service times, financial incentives, coverage decisions or client outcomes.

A reusable generation brief

Review before publication

Check audience fit, factual support, channel accuracy, terminology, CTA destination, accessibility and privacy. A successful AI processing job is not editorial approval. Native workflow review, CMS editorial approval and website publication are separate states.

Challenge the kit

Test requests for a guaranteed approval, an unsupported discount, the wrong state rule and an invented service promise. A useful draft should omit the unsupported claim and explain the missing evidence in the internal reviewer questions. These tests belong in the handoff evidence, not on agent-facing pages.

BRAND KNOWLEDGE / 10

Ownership and implementation

# Ownership and implementation

Keep the native kit, content and code in sync.

Native SitecoreAI setup

Create the requested kit in Design > Brand kits, upload this PDF, review processed knowledge and generated sections, add the glossary deliberately, and assign the reviewed kit to the portal site. Select the same kit when preparing the agency-growth campaign. Record the native kit ID, owner and site association. [SC01, SC02]

Two deliverables, one source of direction

A Brand Kit informs content generation; it does not automatically apply CSS or install a logo. Developers must map the selected colors, typography and assets into versioned application tokens and components. Marketers own reusable guidance, campaign language and editorial review in Sitecore.

Update and handoff

Assign a brand/content owner and a technical owner. On change, update the source date and version, review affected copy, refresh the native kit and record any component-token changes. Retain previous versions and native IDs in the handoff record. We assembled this sample Brand Kit for the Liberty Mutual Agent Portal workshop.
